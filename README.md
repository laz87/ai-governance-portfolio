# AI Governance Portfolio

> **Author:** laz87 | **Location:** Nairobi, Kenya | **Focus:** AI Risk, Compliance, and Responsible AI

## Overview

This repository contains AI governance artifacts — risk assessments, policies, compliance mappings, and due diligence checklists — designed for organizations adopting AI systems. These documents align with Kenya's AI Bill 2026, the EU AI Act, and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF).

## Table of Contents

- [AI Risk Assessment Template](ai-risk-assessment.md) — Comprehensive risk assessment framework for AI systems using NIST AI RMF
- [AI Acceptable Use Policy](ai-acceptable-use-policy.md) — Organizational policy for responsible AI adoption by employees
- [Vendor AI Due Diligence Checklist](vendor-ai-due-diligence.md) — Pre-adoption evaluation checklist for third-party AI tools
- [AI Compliance Mapping](ai-compliance-mapping.md) — Cross-reference of Kenya AI Bill 2026, EU AI Act, and NIST AI RMF controls

## Frameworks Referenced

| Framework | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **NIST AI RMF** | Govern, Map, Measure, Manage — US National Institute of Standards and Technology |
| **EU AI Act** | Risk-based regulatory framework for AI in the European Union |
| **Kenya AI Bill 2026** | Proposed legislation establishing AI governance in Kenya |
| **Kenya Data Protection Act 2019** | Personal data protection requirements applicable to AI systems |
| **ISO/IEC 42001** | International standard for AI management systems |

## How to Use

1. **Risk Assessment:** Use the template to evaluate any AI system before deployment
2. **Policy:** Adapt the acceptable use policy for your organization's context
3. **Due Diligence:** Run the checklist before procuring any third-party AI tool
4. **Compliance Mapping:** Use the matrix to ensure your controls meet regulatory requirements
